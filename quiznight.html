Quiz Night

Ocelot Bar · Written, produced, and hosted by Mihir Kelkar

Quiz Night is a monthly themed trivia night I write, produce, and host at Ocelot Bar. Each night gets a title, like “Titanic,” “Groundhog Day,” or “Endless Summer,” that works as a lens, not a confine. Five categories, two shout-it-out shot rounds to reset the room, and a doubling round: before the first question, every team circles one category, and whatever they score there counts twice.

I run all of it: writing and fact-checking every question, cutting the music clips, building the decks and picture rounds, directing the room, and landing the show in ninety minutes.

What follows is a selection of questions and greatest hits from the past year, for your eyes.
